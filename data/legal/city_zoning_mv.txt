CITY ZONING AND RENTAL REGULATIONS - MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIFORNIA

ZONING CLASSIFICATIONS:
- R-3 (Multi-family Residential): Allows up to 4 units per lot
- R-2 (Two-family Residential): Duplex and accessory dwelling units
- Mixed-use zones permit ground-floor commercial with residential above

SHORT-TERM RENTAL ORDINANCES:
- Maximum 90 days per year for unhosted short-term rentals
- Hosted rentals (owner present) allowed up to 180 days annually
- Registration required with city planning department
- Transient occupancy tax (TOT) of 12% applies to all STR income
- Violations: $500 first offense, $1000 subsequent violations

RENTAL RESTRICTIONS:
- Rent stabilization ordinance covers properties built before 1995
- Maximum annual rent increase: 5% or CPI + 5%, whichever is lower
- Just-cause eviction protections for tenants with 12+ months tenancy
- Security deposits limited to 2 months rent maximum

PARKING REQUIREMENTS:
- Minimum 1.5 parking spaces per dwelling unit
- Electric vehicle charging infrastructure required for new developments
- Bicycle parking minimum 1 space per unit
- Guest parking must be clearly marked and accessible

BUILDING CODES:
- Seismic retrofitting required for buildings constructed before 1980
- Energy efficiency standards: Title 24 compliance mandatory
- Fire safety: Sprinkler systems required in buildings over 3 stories
- Accessibility: ADA compliance required for common areas

PERMIT REQUIREMENTS:
- Building permits required for structural modifications over $5,000
- Electrical work over $1,000 requires licensed contractor
- Plumbing modifications require city inspection
- Architectural review required for exterior changes in historic districts

PROPERTY TAXES:
- Base rate: 1.25% of assessed value
- Additional assessments for infrastructure improvements
- Senior exemptions available for homeowners 65+ with income under $45,000
