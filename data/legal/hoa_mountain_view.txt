HOA RULES AND RESTRICTIONS - MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIFORNIA

APPLIANCE RESTRICTIONS:
- In-unit washer and dryer installations are prohibited in all units
- Residents must use shared laundry facilities on floors 2 and 4
- Installation of portable washing machines is strictly forbidden
- Violations result in $200 fine and immediate removal requirement

PARKING REGULATIONS:
- Each unit allocated one assigned parking space
- Guest parking limited to 2 hours maximum
- No street parking expansion allowed without HOA board approval
- Electric vehicle charging stations require prior approval and installation fee

SHORT-TERM RENTALS:
- Airbnb and similar platforms limited to 90 days per calendar year
- Host must register with HOA and provide proof of insurance
- No rentals under 30 days without special approval
- Violations result in $500 fine per incident

NOISE AND OCCUPANCY:
- Quiet hours enforced 10 PM to 7 AM weekdays, 11 PM to 8 AM weekends
- Maximum occupancy: 2 persons per bedroom + 1 additional person
- No commercial activities or home businesses allowed

PROPERTY MODIFICATIONS:
- Balcony modifications require architectural review committee approval
- Window treatments must be approved by HOA
- No satellite dishes or external antennas without approval
- Landscaping changes require pre-approval

ASSESSMENT FEES:
- Monthly HOA fee: $485 per unit
- Special assessments may be levied for major repairs
- Late payment fee: $50 after 15-day grace period
